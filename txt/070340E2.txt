## 课程简介

International Economics is the fundamental course for bachelor students majoring in International Economics and Trade. International Economics is a compulsory major course in the curriculum of International Economics and Trade. 

International economics is concerned with the effects upon economic activity of international differences in productive resources and consumer preferences and the international institutions that affect them. It seeks to explain the patterns and consequences of transactions and interactions between the inhabitants of different countries. International trade studies goods-and-services flows across international boundaries from supply-and-demand factors, economic integration, international factor movements, and policy variables such as tariff rates and trade quotas. International monetary economics and macroeconomics studies money and macro flow across countries. International political economy from international relations studies issues and impacts from for example international conflicts, international negotiations, and international sanctions; national security and economic nationalism; and international agreements and observance.

Preliminary course: Microeconomics and Macroeconomics.

## 教学目的与要求

This course is dedicated to realize the following training objectives: comprehensive analysis tools and methods of economics, statistics and econometrics, combining macro analysis and micro analysis, qualitative analysis and quantitative analysis, static analysis and dynamic analysis, and comprehensively and systematically explain the international economics theories. At the same time, it pays attention to the integration with the reality of international economic development, to cultivating students" skills on comprehensive analysis and problem-solving, and to forming a solid foundation for students" follow-up professional course study.

Engineering knowledge: Be able to apply mathematics in deducing international economics models and generate professional knowledge in the field of international trade and economics.

Problem Analysis: Be able to apply the basic international trade and finance models and knowledge to identify, express, and analyze practical issues.

Design/development solutions: Be able to design solutions to decision-making problems in the professional field of international trade, and reflect the sense of innovation in the design process, taking into account social, health, safety, legal, cultural and environmental factors.

Research: Be able to study decision-making issues in the professional field of international trade based on scientific principles and using scientific methods, including obtaining, analyzing and interpreting data, and obtaining reasonable and effective conclusions through information synthesis.

Environment and Sustainable Development: Able to understand and evaluate the impact of professional practice on decision-making issues in the field of international trade on the sustainable development of the environment and society.

Professional norms: Have humanities and social science literacy, a sense of social responsibility, be able to understand and abide by the professional ethics and norms of international trade in professional practice, and perform responsibilities.

认识中国在国际经济中的重要积极作用，了解中国为构建“人类命运共同体”所作出的努力。
