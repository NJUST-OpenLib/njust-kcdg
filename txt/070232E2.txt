## 课程介绍【从本课程依托学科及专业背景引出教学内容（具体到章），及修学该课程所需的预备知识等；字数300-500字】

International Finance is one of the core courses of the major "International Economics and Trade". With the development of international flow of commodities, capital, labor and other factors, the interdependence of a country"s economy and the world economy has increased, and international finance introduces the goal of internal and external equilibrium under the open economy from the perspective of money and finance. Besides, this course plays an important role in cultivating talents with extensive knowledge and high quality to meet the requirements of nowadays society. The main content of this course includes the following chapters: International Monetary System, Balance of Payments, Exchange Rate, International Financial Market, International Financial Regulation.

The prerequisite courses for this course are: Microeconomics, Macroeconomics, and Finance.

## 课程目标【学生修学本课程后，从要求掌握的科学方法、具备的创新能力等方面进行概述，同时简要说明本课程涵盖的课程思政目标，字数控制在300字】

Through the implementation of each teaching unit of this course, students can master the basic knowledge, basic theories and basic practices of international finance, and have complete knowledge of engaging in foreign economic and trade and financial activities. Students are required to integrate theory with practice, be able to understand and explain the main practical issues in international finance, grasp the development trend of international finance, and cultivate the ability to independently analyze and solve international monetary and financial problems.

Basic requirements: (1) Master the principle; (2) Understand the operating mechanism; (3) Analyze international financial trends. "

- 第1章 思政融入点："By explaining the new changes in the composition of SDR and the development of RMB internationalization, let international students understand the important role played by China in the international monetary system in recent years. Let international students understand China, respect China and be willing to cooperate with China when they go back home and get employed in relative positions. "
- 第3章 思政融入点：Through the explanation of various influencing factors of exchange rates, students will deeply understand the close relationship between international finance and international political economy. In the current extremely complex international environment, students are taught to look at and analyze problems with comprehensive and critical thinking. Let international students understand the effort China made for financial stability in our area.
- 第4章 思政融入点：Through the analysis of the characteristics and roles of the international financial market, students can understand the important position of the international financial market in the process of international economic globalization and integrated development, cultivate students' ability to innovate and practice.
