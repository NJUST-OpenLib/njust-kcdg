## 课程目标

1. Understand the basic concepts of international trade, foreign trade, in terms of therories and policies
2. Understand the various business methods of China's foreign trade
3. Get to know and analyze China's achievements,challenges, and principles regarding its foreign trade
4. Memorize the related specialized English vocabulary in this course, and learn to express ideas and thoughts in academic written English
