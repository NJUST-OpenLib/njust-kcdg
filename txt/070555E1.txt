## 课程介绍【从本课程依托学科及专业背景引出教学内容（具体到章），及修学该课程所需的预备知识等；字数300-500字】

"本课程主要从全球供应链管理的角度介绍国际物流的基本知识、方法、特点和规则。包括国际物流、国际运输、国际物流空间格局、国际贸易、国际物流网络系统、国际物流信息系统、物流决策、采购、营销、库存、仓储和包装的介绍和运作、物流/供应链管理以及供应链解决方案。通过本课程的学习，学生应了解国际物流的基本知识，培养其操作能力，以及全球供应链管理的基本知识。本课程将加强学生对国际物流的理解，提高他们的实践能力。

先修课程：国际贸易惯例；国际贸易等。

考试：课堂评估和期末笔试。

分数将通过班级评估（40%）和期末笔试（60%）计算。

This course introduces the basic knowledge, methods, characteristics and rules of international logistics, maily from the perspective of global supply chain management. It Includes the introduction and operation of international logistics, international transportation, the spatial patterns of international logistics, international trade, international logistics network system, international logistics information system, logistic decision-making, procurement, marketing, warehouse management, storage and packaging, logistics/supply-chain management, as well as supply-chain solutions. Through the studying of this course, students should know the basic knowledge of international logistics and develop their operation abilities, as well as the basic knowledge of global supply chain management. This course will strengthen the students" understanding of international logistics and improve their practical abilities.

Prerequisite courses: International Trade Practices; International Trade etc..

Exam: Class-based assessment and final written examination.

The score will be calculated by Class-based assessment (40%) and final written examination (60%)."

- 第7章 思政融入点：通过讲述库存安全管理的重要性，培养学生的安全防范意识、责任意识和职业素养。引导学生养成认真负责的工作态度，增强学生的责任担当，有安全防范意识、大局意识和核心意识。培养学生遵守职业道德和职业规范，遵纪守法。
