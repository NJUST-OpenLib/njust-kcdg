## 课程目标

1. To understand the natures of cross-cultural communication.
2. To be aware of the relativity of culture, and the diversity of the ways of living.
3. To be familiar with the specific cultural differences in communicative behavior including verbal and nonverbal communication, value orientations, interpersonal relationships, and business communication.
4. To enhance cross-cultural adaptability. "

## 课程简介

Cross-cultural (or intercultural) understanding and sensitivity have become a very important need for us in today’s globalized world. Effective intercultural communication skills are important in a multicultural society, particularly for those individuals who would be in positions that require effective management of cultural conflicts. The unique features of multicultural education and society provide very good opportunities for students to develop an appreciation for cultural differences and effective intercultural communication skills. It is hoped that this will help students understand their own communication problems so that they will be in a better position to find solutions to these problems. Prerequisite(s): English, Management. "

## 教学目的与要求

This course focuses on fundamental conceptual and practical dimensions of intercultural communication in everyday life in social interactions in multicultural environments. It aims at developing an individual’s intellectual appreciation for cultural differences and sensitivity regarding intercultural interactions.

## 教学内容

Cultural shock
Universal Systems and Cultural Values
Language and Oral Communication
Nonverbal Communication
Global Etiquette
Business and Social Customs

## 课程思政

Let international students learn Chinese Culture, enhance their understanding and fondness of chinese Culture.
Prepare international students with knowledge of Chinese nonverbal communication methods for future cooperation with Chinese corporations.
Equip international students with Chinese business etiquettes for better doing business with Chinese corporations in the future.
