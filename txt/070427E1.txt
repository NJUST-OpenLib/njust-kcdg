## 课程目标

1. Discuss basic concepts, conceptual frameworks, and some broader contexts of international trade in services;
2. Analyze and discuss examples of international trade in services;
3. Develop an understanding of the strategies of international trade in services.

## 课程简介

International Trade in Services is one compulsory course for the undergraduate major of international economy and trade. The course includes 7 chapters. Chapter 1 introduces basic concepts of international trade in services, Chapter 2 introduces some related theories of service and service industry， Chapter 3 introduces service industry transnational management, Chapter 4 introduces theory of international trade in services, Chapter 5 introduces policy of international trade in services, Chapter 6 introduces international service trade agreement, Chapter 7 introduces international service trade classification. Generally speaking, International Trade in Services is a practical course, which mainly introduces the basic knowledge and skills, including definition, environment theory and practices of international trade in services.

## 教学目的与要求

International trade in services is one compulsory course for the undergraduate major of international economy and trade. The task of the course is to combine international trade in services theory with international trade in services practice.The course introduces the basic knowledge and skills of international trade in services, cultivates and improves the students" ability to solve problems of international trade in services activities, so that after graduation they can better adapt to the international trade in services practice and management. After completion of the course students should learn how to write clearly and concisely, learn effectively, think critically and creatively, and work productively.

## 课程思政

take Huawei's telecommunication service in some countries as example to show the concept of community of mankind
take China's political environment as example to show the priority of our society system for foreign service corporations business in China
take the transportation as example to show the importance of the initiative of Belt and Road
