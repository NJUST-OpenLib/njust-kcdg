## 课程目标

1. Enable students to systematically acquire the basic concepts, theories and calculation methods of probability theory and mathematical statistics.
2. Cultivate students' ability to use random thinking and statistical thinking to think about practical problems in their majors, possess a theoretical foundation in analyzing and processing data with randomness, and be able to use statistical methods and software to analyze and process data.
3. Cultivate students' abstract and rigorous logical thinking and reasoning abilities, cultivate their exploratory spirit, scientific research literacy, and the ability to learn independently.

## 课程简介

“Probability and statistics”is a course that study and reveal statistical laws in random phenomena. Probability and statistics are closely related to reality, and they are widely used in natural sciences, technological sciences, social sciences, military, and industrial and agricultural production. They cover almost all scientific and technological fields and are the basic methods and tools for analyzing and solving problems with random characteristics in various disciplines. Probability and Statistics, as a compulsory public basic course for undergraduate students majoring in science and engineering at our university, focuses on introducing commonly used and basic concepts and methods of probability statistics in engineering technology. 

The course content includes: basic concepts of probability theory, random variables and their distribution, multi-dimensional random variable and its distribution, numerical characteristics of random variables, laws of large numbers and the central limit theorem, sampling and sampling distributions, parameter estimation, hypothesis testing of the basic parameters ideas and methods. 

The preparatory knowledge for this course is advanced mathematics and linear algebra.

## 教学目的与要求

This course is intended to serve the concept and method of probability theory and mathematical statistics. We provide the basic definitions, theorems and methods of probability theory, present the most frequently used distributions, provide several statistical methods and their applications. We are hopeful that this course will become a useful course for students who might not have a high level of mathematical maturity, and who expect to use the theory and methods in the studies in their own fields.
