## 课程介绍【从本课程依托学科及专业背景引出教学内容（具体到章），及修学该课程所需的预备知识等；字数300-500字】

Graduation Thesis is an important teaching and practical procedure. It is a kind of comprehensive test of solving both practical and management problems independently in international trade by utilizing the professional knowledge that the students have learned. Graduation Thesis includes the following tasks: (1)Pre-work： Tt contains Graduation Thesis Topic selection; Graduation Thesis Proposal and literature review; （2）Graduation Thesis which includes topic, abstract, keywords, body, references, and tables and or figures related to the thesis；（3）Thesis Defense. The Preparatory knowledge for the Graduation Thesis includes (1) Theoretical knowledge of international trade; (2) Econometric analysis methods; (3) Perfect English, especially for those students whose native language is not English.

## 课程目标【学生修学本课程后，从要求掌握的科学方法、具备的创新能力等方面进行概述，同时简要说明本课程涵盖的课程思政目标，字数控制在300字】

The purpose of the graduation thesis for those undergraduates major in International Economics and Trade: (1) To cultivate the students" preliminary ability to carry out scientific research under the guidance of the tutor; (2) To make students understand the reality of international economy and trade and increase the perceptual knowledge of international economic activities through certain social practice; (3) To write the thesis through the collection of relevant literature and master the research trends in the field; (4) To cultivate the students" comprehensive analysis ability and writing ability through the writing of graduation thesis; (5) To cultivate the student"s ability to express their expression and answer questions through the defense.
