## 课程介绍【从本课程依托学科及专业背景引出教学内容（具体到章），及修学该课程所需的预备知识等；字数300-500字】

国际金融市场和机构课程是为有兴趣从事金融市场和金融服务业的学生而开设的。本课程探讨了国际金融市场的作用、国际金融市场的种类及特点、不同类型金融市场中交易的工具、银行及其主要竞争对手在竞争日益激烈的金融服务市场中提供的服务，以及对市场中参与主体和产品的监管。本课程主要介绍现代国际金融市场和机构的基本原理、主要方法和最新发展。
课程主要内容包括如下章节：全球视角的金融体系；国际货币体系；利率；货币市场与央行；债券市场与股票市场；金融财务报表；存贷款管理；金融监管。
本课程所需的预修课程有：金融学、国际金融。
The course of international financial markets and institutions is designed for students interested in pursuing careers in or learning more about the financial markets and financial services industry. It explores the role of financial markets, the instruments traded in financial markets, the services that banks and their principal competitors offer in an increasingly competitive financial-services marketplace, and the regulations on them.？ This course mainly focuses on the basic principles, main methods and newly development of modern international financial markets and institutions.
Prerequisite(s): Finance, International Finance

## 课程目标【学生修学本课程后，从要求掌握的科学方法、具备的创新能力等方面进行概述，同时简要说明本课程涵盖的课程思政目标，字数控制在300字】

本课程的目的是为学生提供金融市场的基本知识，并对国际银行和其他金融机构在国际金融市场中的地位和职能分析。通过学习本课程，学生将熟悉促进全球贸易和投资活动的关键金融市场和工具；了解金融机构的功能及其面临的风险；不仅掌握基本国际金融领域知识，而且持续关注国际金融领域的热点问题，学会分析复杂的金融问题并做出决策，探索有效应对这些挑战的方法。
The purpose of this course is to provide students with a fundamental knowledge of the functions performed and the roles played by the financial system of financial market, as well as the status and roles of important financial institutions including international banks and other financial-service providers. By studying this course, students will be acquainted with the key financial markets and instruments that facilitate trade and investment activity on a global scale, understand what the financial institutions do for people and the risks they present. Students can learn to analyze financial problems as well as making financial decisions. Students can also examine the different risk exposures that confront financial-service providers today, discover ways to deal with these challenges efficiently and effectively, and keep focusing on the international events happening in the field of international financial markets and institutions.
