## 课程目标

1. Understand what economics as a discipline deals with; what the main economic principles are.
2. Apply economic models to analyze a situation in terms of economics.
3. Interpret charts, graphs, and tables and use the information to make informed judgments.
4. Communicate their ideas and understanding of economic issues using written, verbal and visual expression.

## 课程简介

This is one of two introductory courses in economics. This course studies the economy as a whole. It explains how economic events affect many households, firms, and markets simultaneously. It provides students with the opportunity to develop and refine their critical thinking/problem solving skills from a social science perspective.Students are required to learn microeconomics before taking the course.

## 教学目的与要求

This course specifically seeks to develop students’ critical thinking and problem-solving skills, especially as these apply to the analysis of macroeconomic theory and policy. It aims to develop students’ abilities to construct and sustain an argument using the phrases and concepts that economists use in their deliberations. A theoretical framework is developed in which students acquire an understanding of how economic agents interact and by doing so develop the literacy and verbal communication skills necessary for presenting arguments of an economic nature.
