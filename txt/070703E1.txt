## 课程介绍【从本课程依托学科及专业背景引出教学内容（具体到章），及修学该课程所需的预备知识等；字数300-500字】

"This course fully reflects the relevant international business customs and rules, such as trade terms, international transport, international transport insurance, international settlement and arbitrations etc. This course provides students with a broad understanding of international business customs & practices. Students will develop the analytical skills needed to understand and maneuver business in an American and International setting.

Prerequisite courses: International Trade, International Trade Practice, International Finance etc..

Exam: Class-based assessment and final written examanition.

The score will be calculated by Class-based assessment (40%) and final written examanition (60%)."

## 课程目标【学生修学本课程后，从要求掌握的科学方法、具备的创新能力等方面进行概述，同时简要说明本课程涵盖的课程思政目标，字数控制在300字】

"This course attempts to introduce a series of international customs and rules for international economic activities. The course aims to provide the students with a fundamental understanding of the international economic customs and rules. By studying this course, the student will be acquainted with every segment of customs and practices for import and export and be able to develop their abilities to settle the international business disputes independently.

Specifically, the students are expected to have the following abilities after learning this course:

1. Master the basic customes and rules related to international trade and business;

2. Use the knowledge to analyze some practical problems;

3. Employ the knowledge to make some solutions;

4. Be professional in related jobs.

"
