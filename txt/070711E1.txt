## 课程介绍【从本课程依托学科及专业背景引出教学内容（具体到章），及修学该课程所需的预备知识等；字数300-500字】

This course builds on the course Introductory International Economics and trade. It provides deeper understanding of the determinants and effects of international trade and Foreign Direct Investment and of exchange rate determination. In addition, it elaborates on the desirability of international trade policy and of macroeconomic policy in an open economy. This will be accomplished by taking a closer and a more formal look at the main models. The course also extends the theoretical and empirical framework by focusing on topics such as preferential trade arrangements, the desirability of different trade policies and exchange rate systems in view of a country"s stage of development.Students are required to learn Macroeconomics, Microeconomics, International Trade, International Finance and International Economics before taking this course. In addition, the basic knowledge in Calculus is also a prerequisite.

## 课程目标【学生修学本课程后，从要求掌握的科学方法、具备的创新能力等方面进行概述，同时简要说明本课程涵盖的课程思政目标，字数控制在300字】

This course emphasizes both mathematical models and empirical studies of how well those models fit actual data. Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to work on understanding the economic logic (intuition)

1. To know the key facts relating to international trade and international macroeconomics.
2. To employ models to analyze the phenomena in international economics and trade.
