## 课程介绍【从本课程依托学科及专业背景引出教学内容（具体到章），及修学该课程所需的预备知识等；字数300-500字】

International investment is a kind of economic behavior that investors transform their own money capital and industrial capital into physical assets, intangible assets or financial assets by transnational flow, and achieve added value through transnational business. The main body of international investment is transnational corporation. This course can be divided into 3 parts: the first is the knowledge of international investment and transnational corporation theory, the second part is the knowledge of transnational corporations management, the third part is international investment management.

## 课程目标【学生修学本课程后，从要求掌握的科学方法、具备的创新能力等方面进行概述，同时简要说明本课程涵盖的课程思政目标，字数控制在300字】

International Investment & Transnational Corporations is one compulsory course for the undergraduate major of international economy and trade. The task of the course is to combine international investment & transnational corporation theory with international investment & transnational corporations practice. The course introduces the basic knowledge and skills of international investment & transnational corporation, cultivates and improves the students" ability to solve problems of international investment & transnational corporation activities, so that after graduation they can better adapt to the international investment & transnational corporation practice and management. After completion of the course students should learn how to write clearly and concisely, learn effectively, think critically and creatively, and work productively.

## 课程目标分条说明【可不囊括所有的毕业要求点，一条课程目标可对应多个毕业要求】

1. Discuss basic concepts, conceptual frameworks, and some broader contexts of international investment & transnational corporations
2. Analyze and discuss examples of international investment & transnational corporations
3. Develop an understanding of the strategies of international investment & transnational corporations.
