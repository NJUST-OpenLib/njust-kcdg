## 课程目标

1. Understand certain concepts, theories of international trade
2. Grasp policies, measurements, treaties and agreements, main organizations of international trade and know their influences on it
3. Get skills of case analyzing, learn to think all-around, carefully and objectively from the point of international trade regulations, laws, terms and policies.

## 课程简介

International trade is focused on fundamental knowledge like theories, policies, etc. on international trade. This course will provide an overall understanding of international trade development and functions, international market formation, international trade process, factors influencing international trade, international trade organizations, and so on. The course will provide a unified framework to address these issues and to study effects of trade policies, measures and organizations on international trade. These theories and analytic tools will be used to understand the trade conditions and conflicts between China and other countries.

## 教学目的与要求

This is one of the core courses for international economic and trade major. This course provides students with a broad understanding of international trade concepts, theories, policies, measures, regulations, organizations, etc. It will give students an overall understanding of international trade and develop the analytical skills of international trade cases according to certain trade treaties and policies. Meanwhile, by learning the students will grasp usage of different trade terms, trade rules and measures as well as strengthen English listening, speaking and communication skills.This course is a propaedeutic introduction of international trade concepts, theories, policies, measures, treaties and agreements, organizations and so on. It aims to help students who intend to work in fields relevant to international trade or business to get an overview of international trade and get well prepared for further study in the major.
