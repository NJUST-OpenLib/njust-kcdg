## 课程简介

This course mainly introduces programmable logic controllers and focuses on the teaching of some common technologies in PLC. The main contents of this course includes the basic concepts of programmable logic controllers. It introduces some definitions and the state-of-art of programmable logic controllers. The principles of programmable logic controller are presented and discussed in detail. and number system and codes in PLC are explained. Some concepts of input and output devices will be introduced in detail. How data will be processed in PLC and what function will be used to realize math and logical operation are explained. Some methods of programming in PLC are shown in detail, such as Ladder diagrams, instruction lists, sequential function charts, etc. Some techniques are also given, for example, how to use timers, counters, etc. and how to realize some special program structure will be introduced, such as jump structure and subroutines program. Finally, some examples of PLC applications are shown and discussed sufficiently and some methods of solving trouble problems are given. This course is mostly based on the lectures, combined with multimedia PPTs and student’s class discussions and exercises. The assessment of this course is adopted on a paper about special topics, and the students final notes are calculated on the basis of the score of a paper and the usual results.

## 教学目的与要求

Course Objectives:

To make students understand the origin of programmable logic controllers, how programmable logic controllers work, how to install and setup a PLC, how to program, what PLC programming languages include and what ladder program is, how to use timers and counters etc.

General Requirements:

After finishing this course, students should be able:

1.Understand the historical evolution of PLC, and their importance to our industrial process.

2.Understand the fundamental principles of programmable logic controller, such as its components, the difference between PLCs and computers etc.

3.Understand how PLC works, and what its number system and codes.

4.Understand how programming in PLC and what PLC programming languages include.

5.Understand how to use timers, counters and some math instruction.

6.Understand how to design and realize an application using PLC.
