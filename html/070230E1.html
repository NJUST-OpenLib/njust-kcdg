课程介绍【从本课程依托学科及专业背景引出教学内容（具体到章），及修学该课程所需的预备知识等；字数300-500字】

The course International Technology is for students majoring in international economics and trade. Both as a new type of business practice and a new academic discipline, this course lays particular emphasis on the direct mobility of technology, one of the most important and special production factors. The course contents mainly includes the basic theories and practices of international technology trade. Besides relevant policies and legislations, the teaching focuses are objects & means of international technology trade, the procedure of international technology trade’s deal negotiation, the technology trade contract, the price & payment of technology transaction, the taxes and duties of technology trade, etc. The students should have the knowledge of international trade & practices of international trade prior to the study of this course. The required courses for this course include international trade, international finance and international investment and transnational corporations etc.

课程目标【学生修学本课程后，从要求掌握的科学方法、具备的创新能力等方面进行概述，同时简要说明本课程涵盖的课程思政目标，字数控制在300字】

The main function of this course is to enable the students to amass theoretical knowledge and improve their professional ability on the field of international technology trade, master the general procedures for different international technology trade, grasp the basic knowledge and skills for international technology trade negotiation,to sign and fulfilling a contract for international technology and lay the foundation for their future business career.

课程目标分条说明【可不囊括所有的毕业要求点，一条课程目标可对应多个毕业要求】

  • 1、Understand the basic concepts of international Technology Trade
  • 2、Understand the various methods of international Technology
  • 3、Understand the key contents of international technology trade
  • 4、Memorize the related customs and laws related to international technology trade
