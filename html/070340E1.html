课程简介

International economics is concerned with the effects upon economic activity of international differences in productive resources and consumer preferences and the international institutions that affect them. It seeks to explain the patterns and consequences of transactions and interactions between the inhabitants of different countries, including trade, investment and migration. International trade studies goods-and-services flows across international boundaries from supply-and-demand factors, economic integration, international factor movements, and policy variables such as tariff rates and trade quotas. International finance studies the flow of capital across international financial markets, and the effects of these movements on exchange rates. International monetary economics and macroeconomics studies money and macro flow across countries. International political economy from international relations studies issues and impacts from for example international conflicts, international negotiations, and international sanctions; national security and economic nationalism; and international agreements and observance.

教学目的与要求

This is a required course for the specialty of International Economics & trade and Economics. and this is also a basic course for majors of economics as well as a core course for majors of international economics and trade, aiming at the students’ mastery of the basic concepts, theories and assessment of international trade theory and policy as well as international finance theory and policy. For example, balance of payments adjustment mechanism, exchange rates and foreign exchange market, economic globalization theory and so on. The study of this course helps students know better about the operation rules of international trade, investment, and finance in the global economy, develops the student’s ability to analyze, summarize and solve practical problems.
