课程介绍【从本课程依托学科及专业背景引出教学内容（具体到章），及修学该课程所需的预备知识等；字数300-500字】

"International Business Simulation Practice" is a required course for the major of international Economics and trade in the discipline of applied economics. It is a practical course that combines the theory of international trade practice with practical operation, and belongs to an important part of the teaching plan of international economics and trade major. Based on the explanation of each link of international trade practice operation, this course guides students to operate on computer and complete each link of international trade practice in the practical operation software system, which can help students to effectively consolidate and strengthen their mastery of relevant knowledge. At the same time, this course focuses on the cultivation of students" strain ability, independent judgment and ability to skillfully use theoretical knowledge to solve practical problems. The teaching contents include: system introduction and system login, "L/C+CIF" business process, "L/C+CFR" business process, "D/P+FOB" business process, "T/T+CIF" business process, "T/T+CFR" business process, "D/A+FOB" business process, document making and risk management.

课程目标【学生修学本课程后，从要求掌握的科学方法、具备的创新能力等方面进行概述，同时简要说明本课程涵盖的课程思政目标，字数控制在300字】

The main function of this course is to enable students to verify and consolidate the theoretical knowledge of offering and contract signing on the basis of mastering the basic knowledge of international trade practice, and deepen the understanding and application of the operation process of import and export trade. Through the teaching of this course, students should have the ability to solve and cope with practical problems related to import and export trade. International business simulation practice curriculum not only pay attention to training students" practice of international trade related professional knowledge and skills, and in the simulated operation each link of teaching, strengthen the cultivation of students" socialist core values, achieve the whole education and comprehensive education, education of students not only to improve their professional level, but must work hard in the moral cultivation and to become a socialist builder and successor with all-round development of morality, intelligence, body and beauty.

课程目标分条说明【可不囊括所有的毕业要求点，一条课程目标可对应多个毕业要求】

  • Master the key points of import and export practice of both sides
  • Master the business operation links of banking, customs, commodity inspection, verification and other departments
  • Make a comprehensive analysis of the complications that may arise in the process of international trade
