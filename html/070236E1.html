课程简介

This course is a professional course for those students majored in International Economics and Trade. It is a practice-oriented curriculum. The course focuses on the discussion of the international sales of goods from the following four steps: preparation for international trade; international business negotiation; conclusion of the written contract and its contents; performance or execution of the international contract for the sales of goods. The contents are as follows: (1) Introduction; (2) Preparation for international trade; (3) International business negotiation and the structure and contents of the written contract; (4) Subject of the contract; (5) Price; (6) Transportation; (7) Insurance; (8) Payment;(9) General Terms in the S/C; (10) Performance of the contract. Preliminary course: International Trade; International Finance

教学目的与要求

It aims to provide the students with a fundamental understanding of the procedure of the import and the export. By studying this course, students will be acquainted with every segment of import and export activities. Students will be able to conduct negotiations independently in international business transactions, to take precautions against breach of contracts and to settle the claims according to relevant laws/acts and international customs and practices. Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to: (1) Have a general understanding on the procedure of import and export; (2) Be able to conclude a contract for the international sales of goods; (3) Understand the latest international customs and practices, conventions and relevant laws of international business; (4) Be able to draft and know how to check the documents needed in importation and exportation. (5) Be able to execute the contract for the international sales of goods.
