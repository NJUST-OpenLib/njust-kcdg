课程简介

Linear algebra is a basic course in colleges and universities. It mainly studies the linear theory of finite-dimensional spaces. With the universal applications of computer technology and the rapid development of high-tech, this theory increasingly penetrated into all fields of science. As a basis for research in various disciplines, linear algebra course needs to be more strengthened and improved. This course consists of seven chapters: Chapter 1 starts with vectors and matrices. Chapter 2 begins the algebra of matrices: using elimination to solve linear equations. Chapter 3 is linear algebra at the best level: subspaces. Chapter 4 introduces orthogonality and projections. Chapter 5 gives formulas of determinants. Chapter 6 tells us how to diagonalize a symmetric marix using its eigenvalues and eigenvectors. Chapter 7 explains the linear transformation approach. Preliminary knowledge such as vector algebra and space analytic geometry is required before learning this course. Classroom lecturing with moderate practice courses is the main teaching method and written examinations are the primary means of assessment.
