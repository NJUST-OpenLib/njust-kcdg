课程简介

International Business Negotiation is designed for students (international students in particular)majored in International Economics and Trade. The purpose of this course is to help students to understand the theory and process of international business negotiation and to provide students with opportunities to develop individual negotiation skills for commercial transactions across countries. The course is designed to be relevant to negotiation situations, in the field of international business in particular.

教学目的与要求

International Business Negotiation presents negotiation theories and communication negotiation, individual differences and cultural implications on international business negotiation. In addition to the theory and exercises presented in class, students practice negotiating with role-playing simulations. Upon completion of this course, students will 1. Understand the characteristics and utilities of international business negotiation; 2. Be acquainted with two core approaches to negotiation: the basic dynamics of competitive bargaining and the basic dynamics of integrative negotiation; learn a variety of strategies employed by negotiators in the international business arena; 3. Increase awareness of the negotiation process in general including the psychological sub-process; 4. Understand the impact of culture on international business negotiations; 5. Learn how to observe and adapt to communication for business people from different cultures.
